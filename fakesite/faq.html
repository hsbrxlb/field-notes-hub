Frequently asked questions

Get the details right before you order or install.

Where do I start and stop measuring my truck bed?

Measure from the inside face of the front wall to the inside face of the closed tailgate, keeping the tape straight. Record the measurement in inches. Do not add the thickness of the tailgate or the bumper.

Is a 5.7-foot bed the same as 5 feet 7 inches?

No. Decimal feet and feet-plus-inches use different notation. Multiply decimal feet by 12 to convert to inches. A seller's bed-size label can also differ from the measured inside length; use the exact vehicle and product fitment information as well as your measurement.

What vehicle details do I need before choosing a cover?

Have the year, make, model, truck generation, cab configuration, measured bed length and factory bed equipment ready. Check the exact cover option's exclusions and installation guide. Similar truck names or matching nominal bed lengths alone do not confirm fitment.

Can a bed liner or rail system affect a cover installation?

Yes. A liner, track, toolbox or other bed accessory can change the space available for clamps and rails. Compare your setup with the installation instructions for the selected cover, and ask the seller about any conflict before ordering.

Check the mounting space →Link to this answer
What is the difference between cab-length and wheel-to-wheel boards?

Cab-length boards run alongside the cab. Wheel-to-wheel designs extend toward the rear wheel opening, but the extra length does not always include a usable bed step. Check the specific layout and mounting kit rather than relying on the name.

Compare board layouts →Link to this answer
Does a wider step mean an easier step up?

Step width describes the foot surface, not its height above the ground. Entry height depends on the installed step position and the truck's setup. Confirm dimensions for the exact board and vehicle if ease of entry is your priority.

Will every running board include the right mounting kit?

Do not assume so. Some boards require a separately sold vehicle-specific kit. Confirm the box contents and the exact kit needed for your year, cab and truck configuration before ordering.

Check the kit and installation guide →Link to this answer
Can running boards be fitted alongside factory rock rails?

That depends on the selected board and installation guide. Some designs require removal of the original rails. Match the manual to the exact product option and ask the seller when the page and manual are unclear; do not assume one answer applies to every style.

What should I do when product fitment information conflicts?

Pause the purchase and ask the seller to confirm the exact option against your vehicle details and accessories. Include the product link, the conflicting information and your measurements. A broad product title is not a substitute for an option-specific fitment answer.
