Ram 1500 Bed Covers: Classic, New Body, RamBox, and Tailgates

Identify the Ram 1500 body version, bed, RamBox equipment, and tailgate before choosing a tonneau cover and mounting kit.

For a Ram 1500 cover, confirm the body version, bed size, RamBox equipment, and tailgate type as separate items. A listing that includes your model year can still exclude the truck's bed or tailgate configuration.

Identify Classic or the newer body version

Write down the complete model designation from the vehicle documents. If your order requires a Classic-versus-New-Body choice, settle that choice before selecting a bed cover. A registration year alone is not enough when an application distinguishes both versions.

The inspected OEDRO product 5884 page illustrates the issue. Its fitment section separately lists 2009 to 2018 Ram 1500, 2019 to 2024 Classic, and 2019 to 2026 New Body applications for the specified short bed. Those rows should remain separate. The broad combined headline is not permission to apply every listed year to every body version.

If you are uncertain, ask a Ram dealer to identify the vehicle from its documents or VIN. Send that confirmed designation to the cover seller. Do not use a previous owner's informal “new style” description as the final application record.

Keep the bed name and measurement separate

Record the nominal bed size used for your truck and measure the inside cargo box. With the tailgate closed, measure between the inside bulkhead face and the inside tailgate face. The measurement guide explains the endpoints and the difference between a bed-size label and a tape reading.

Save the reading in inches without rounding it into the nearest product title. Different decimal and feet-plus-inches labels can look deceptively similar. If two parts use inconsistent size wording, ask the seller which part matches your measured bed and body configuration.

A short-bed cover that fit another Ram is not automatically suitable. Record the old cover's label and mounting kit if you are transferring or buying it used. Missing brackets or an unidentified variant can make an otherwise familiar cover impossible to verify.

Identify RamBox and ordinary bed accessories separately

Ram describes RamBox as storage bins built into the sides of the bed. Its factory description also shows these separately from the multifunction tailgate. A removable toolbox sitting across the front of the bed is a different accessory and should be named separately.

OEDRO product 5884 excludes RamBox in its inspected application. A matching bed-length label does not override that exclusion. Ask for a cover and mounting system explicitly documented for RamBox if your truck has it.

Photograph both upper bed edges and the storage lids, if present. Include rail caps, a drop-in liner, movable cargo tracks, and any rack. The seller needs to see where its hardware would attach and which openings you still need to use.

Describe how the tailgate opens

Record whether the truck has a conventional drop-down tailgate or a split multifunction tailgate. Ram's factory description explains that the multifunction design can open as side-swinging doors or lower like a conventional tailgate. These are different movements to consider when evaluating a cover.

The OEDRO cover example also excludes a split tailgate. Do not assume that using only the drop-down function removes the exclusion. Hardware or clearance can be relevant even when you leave another function unused.

For a cover documented for your tailgate, ask how to open and close both parts in the correct order. Confirm whether all intended tailgate functions remain available. If the seller cannot identify the exact application, keep that candidate unconfirmed.

Check the complete cargo setup

Decide whether a rack, toolbox, or other rail-mounted accessory must remain. The inspected OEDRO example requires cargo-rack removal for the described installation. A separate rack fitment claim does not establish that it works alongside the cover.

Ask about the folded stack and the space it leaves near the front of the bed. A cover may match the truck yet make a frequently used toolbox awkward to open. Describe your normal cargo and request the approved driving positions for the selected style.

Finish the inquiry with the product link, selected style, body version, nominal size, measured length, RamBox status, tailgate type, and rail photographs. Keep the written answer with the order. For the next buying decision, compare cover opening layouts and cargo routines after the vehicle application has been resolved.

Check your vehicle fit

Confirm the exact year, model and configuration on the product page before ordering.
