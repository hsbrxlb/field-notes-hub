Toyota Tundra Floor Mats: Cab, Powertrain, and Rear-Floor Fit

Identify Double Cab versus CrewMax, record hybrid and rear-floor details, and check the exact liner coverage before ordering Tundra floor mats.

Choose Tundra floor mats from the cabin configuration, including the cab name and rear-floor equipment. Record whether the truck uses i-FORCE or i-FORCE MAX when asking about a liner that reaches under the rear seat. A broad model-year label does not answer every coverage question.

Identify Double Cab or CrewMax

Read the cab name from the vehicle's original paperwork or have a Toyota dealer identify it. Do not use “four doors” as a substitute. Toyota's 2024 Tundra announcement describes both Double Cab and CrewMax as four-door configurations. A seller needs the specific name to identify the relevant interior.

Model year belongs in the same record. A liner advertised for a later generation should not be assumed to fit an earlier truck simply because both say CrewMax. Keep the vehicle year and the complete application visible while comparing products.

Photograph the front footwells and rear floor from both rear doors. Include the area around the center console and the lower seat edges. Those images show useful information that a photo of the outside of the truck cannot.

Record powertrain and rear-floor equipment

Write down i-FORCE or i-FORCE MAX exactly as identified for your truck. Toyota distinguishes the twin-turbo V6 and hybrid powertrain in its Tundra documentation. Do not treat the powertrain name as proof that a particular mat does or does not fit. It is information the liner supplier should use when confirming the full application.

Identify existing storage, an aftermarket organizer, and any liner coverage that extends beneath the rear seat. If a product photograph shows an open area where your vehicle has equipment, ask about that difference. It may reflect a different configuration, a representative image, or a different coverage design.

For a hybrid, locate the battery-cooling information in your own owner's manual. Toyota's hybrid guidance explains that battery cooling uses cabin air intakes and that their locations vary by model. Keep those openings clear according to the vehicle instructions. Do not remove covers or alter equipment under a seat to make a floor liner fit.

Read the brand, coverage, and exclusions

The OEDRO store also carries products labeled YITAMOTOR. The Tundra CrewMax listing for product 7931, for example, identifies YITAMOTOR as the brand and lists a front-and-rear liner set. An item appearing on OEDRO's site should not automatically be described as an OEDRO-branded part.

That inspected listing gives a broad CrewMax application without resolving every hybrid or rear-equipment question. If your purchase depends on coverage beneath the rear seat or around a particular storage arrangement, ask for confirmation for that configuration. A missing exclusion is not the same as a documented approval.

Check which surfaces the set covers: separate front footwells, the center area, the rear footwell, and any extension beneath the seat. A photo showing several pieces does not establish that every piece is included in your selected option. Compare the package list with the option name and part identifier.

Choose coverage you can use and clean

Think about the mess you are trying to manage. Work boots bring grit into the front footwell. Rear passengers may place bags or wet gear against a different edge. Look for photos of the areas that matter to you, rather than judging coverage from an isolated product image.

Consider removal as well as installation. Ask whether an extended rear liner can be removed for cleaning without dismantling an organizer or working around fixed equipment. If cleaning means repeatedly forcing the liner through a narrow gap, a different coverage layout may be more practical.

Do not buy by the shipping-box measurements. They describe packaging, not the installed boundary around seats and trim. Request a dimensioned product drawing or an installed photo when a precise edge position would decide the purchase.

Inspect the delivered set before use

Compare labels and piece count with the order. Remove the old removable mats, position the new set, and use the retention method specified for your vehicle. Keep the driver's pedal area clear and confirm that the rear liner does not obstruct required openings or moving seat components.

If an edge or retention point does not match, photograph it and contact support before trimming. Preserve the unmodified part and packaging while the application is checked. The floor-mat installation guide covers the safety checks to repeat after every cleaning.

Check your vehicle fit

Confirm the exact year, model and configuration on the product page before ordering.
