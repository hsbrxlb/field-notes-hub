What information should I have before ordering an accessory?

Record the vehicle's year, make, model, and exact configuration. For steps, include cab and existing side hardware. For a cover, include bed length, tailgate, rails, and installed cargo accessories. For liners, include cab, seating, flooring, and rear storage. Save the selected product option as well as the page link.

What if the vehicle selector and product description disagree?

Keep the item unconfirmed. Send support the vehicle details, selected option, and the two conflicting statements. Request the correct application for that part number before ordering. Do not choose whichever statement gives the broader fitment range.

Are SuperCab and SuperCrew interchangeable when buying accessories?

Treat them as separate cab designations. Ford identifies them separately, and accessory applications may specify one. Use the exact cab in the listing; door count and a similar-looking vehicle photo do not prove that brackets or interior liners are interchangeable.

Where should I measure a truck bed for a cover?

With the tailgate closed, measure lengthwise between the inside bulkhead face and inside tailgate face. Record inches and photograph both endpoints. If a liner affects what the tape touches, note that. Use the reading alongside the vehicle application and nominal bed name.

Why does my tape reading differ from the advertised bed-size name?

A nominal bed name may differ from the inside dimension. Keep both values instead of rounding the tape reading to match a listing. Also distinguish decimal feet from feet and inches. Ask the seller to resolve a material mismatch before choosing the cover.

Which running-board measurements matter for easier entry?

Compare installed tread height, usable tread depth and width, and its position beneath the door. Ask where the height measurement starts and which tire and suspension setup it describes. Overall length or shipping-box dimensions do not tell you how comfortably you can step into the cab.

Can I put all-weather mats on top of my existing mats?

No. Remove the existing removable mats. Stacking can reduce pedal clearance and leave a mat unsecured. Fit the new driver's mat with the vehicle's intended retention system and check the pedal area before driving.
